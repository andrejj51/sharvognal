## Задание встроенного ImageGen

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one atmospheric landscape illustration for a local Zazerkalye ping-pong club website header, not a UI mockup.
A scene like a lost early-2000s Russian PS1 computer game: low-polygon mossy pine forest with a primitive weathered wooden ping-pong table, worn green tabletop, correctly stretched small net, one small white ping-pong ball and red wooden paddle on table. A tall battered rectangular gold-framed mirror stands behind the table, reflecting a strange blue-purple sky rather than the forest. Tiny red white-spotted mushrooms, blocky stone pillars and grass surround the table. Distant floating rock behind treetops. Uncanny and gently absurd, nostalgic, quiet; NOT a sleek modern fantasy illustration.
Wide composition, table and mirror in right half, darker quiet forest in left half for readable overlaid site heading. Main subject around center-right so a shallow horizontal crop remains useful. Grainy pixelated textures, visibly angular primitive geometry, PS1 texture warping aesthetic, green/moss/ochre palette with muted violet sky. Avoid smooth glossy cartoon shading or photorealism. No characters, no people, no text, no captions, no logo, no watermark, no UI. Landscape 1536x1024.
